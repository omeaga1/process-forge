# Units that ignore their feed are called out

- **A unit whose output doesn't follow from its input is now flagged.** If a designed unit splits its feed by a typed-in number (a share, a recovery or a drain volume that reads nothing from the stream), validation warns that it separates the same fraction of any feed, and says how to derive it from the physics instead.
- **Claude is told to compute, not type in, what a unit makes.** The design instructions now say how: a crystalliser's yield comes from `crystal_yield()` and the solubility curve, a centrifuge or filter reads the solids in its feed, a dryer reads the feed moisture. When you state a target (a yield, a purity), Claude solves for the operating condition that reaches it.
- The built-in crystalliser, centrifuge, filter and dryer pass the new check.
