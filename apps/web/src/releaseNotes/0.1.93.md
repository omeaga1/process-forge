# The filter reads your slurry

- **The solids filter reads the feed's crystals.** It used to assume a typed-in 8% solids whatever you sent it. Now it takes the solids from the stream (the typed value is only a fallback when the feed has no composition).
- **The cake is what you set.** Crystals go to the cake at the capture you set, the cake holds the liquid that makes it the set dryness, and everything dissolved in that liquid goes with it. The filtrate carries the rest.
- Checked on the Epsom line: with 97% capture and a 70% cake, the line delivers 97% and 70%.
