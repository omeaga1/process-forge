# Designed units drawn like the palette's own

- **Units Claude designs are drawn properly.** A new tool, draw_unit_op, draws a designed unit from its contract at the standard of the Standard palette: guarding and internals in the theme's colours, the bottles, cans, cases or pallets it handles, a turret, belt, gantry head or impeller that moves while the line runs, and a tank level that follows the simulation. The contract's own numbers shape it (a filler's valves, a conveyor's lanes, a case's rows and columns), and every unit gets a variant of its own.
- **Claude looks at what it drew.** preview_unit_drawing renders a drawing to an image in this app, so Claude can check and fix it before it reaches your canvas.
- **Start from what is already modelled.** fork_unit_op copies a Standard or Library unit, contract and drawing, for Claude to reshape into what you asked for.
- **Accumulation conveyors you designed count as buffers** in the bottleneck check, and the design brief now gives the stream that actually reaches a unit rather than placeholder figures.
