# Carbonators and cappers that run

- **A gas that dissolves no longer chokes the unit it feeds.** A carbonator's CO2 used to count against its rated flow as gas volume, so 45 kg/h of CO2 (about 100 gal/min of gas) left no room for the beverage and everything vented. The rating now counts liquid only, and the CO2 that dissolves travels on in the beverage as liquid instead of filling the next tank with gas.
- **An assembly unit fed from a feed arrow keeps getting parts.** A capper whose caps came from a feed stopped receiving them once bottles filled its queue, then waited for caps forever. Each inlet now has room of its own.
