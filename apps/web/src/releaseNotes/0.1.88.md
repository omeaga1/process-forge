# The studio tells you where the line stands

- **Status bar** along the bottom of the canvas: empty, incomplete, ready, solved, the run's verdict, or results gone stale after an edit. **F4** (or "N to check") jumps to the next problem and opens that unit.
- **Pipes show their flow before you run.** A liquid line is solved at steady state as you build it, so each pipe reads like "20 gpm · 10 °C" without pressing Run.
- **Hover a pipe** for its stream card: flow, temperature, composition.
- **State bars** under each unit after a run: how long it was busy, starved, blocked or down.
- **Run length** on the toolbar: 30m, 2h, 8h, 24h. A line with a batch unit starts on a shift.
- **Scrub the run**: drag the timeline in the copilot (or arrow keys) and the canvas follows.
- **Export the flowsheet as an image** (Ctrl+K, "Export"): the whole sheet as a PNG with a title block.
- Claude's tools: `solve_steady_state` gives the line's heat and mass balance at steady state, recycles included (MCP 0.21.0).
