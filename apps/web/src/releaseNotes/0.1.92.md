# The dryer and centrifuge read what actually arrives

- **The dryer reads the feed's water.** It used to dry a typed-in "40% moisture" whatever came in. Now it takes the moisture from the stream (the typed value is only a fallback when the feed has no composition), and only water leaves as vapour. Salt and crystals all stay in the product.
- **The dryer's duty follows from its streams.** It is the heat to warm the feed plus the latent heat of the water at the exhaust temperature, so the "stated duty is larger than stream heat" warning no longer appears. Heat losses are now a thermal efficiency, and the burner check uses duty divided by that efficiency.
- **The centrifuge reads the feed's crystals.** Crystals go to the paste at the machine's recovery, and the liquid the paste holds takes everything dissolved in it along, so paste dryness is the set value.
- **A full Epsom line now balances end to end.** Crystalliser, centrifuge and dryer: mass closes and the dryer's energy check is consistent.
- Units that give both a share and recoveries use recoveries when the stream has composition and the share when it does not, so older flowsheets keep working.
