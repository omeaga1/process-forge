# A spec sheet you can read at a glance

- **Every setting shows its range as a gauge.** The bar is the allowed range, coloured green where the design passes, amber where it warns and red where it fails, with a marker at your value. The passing range is written underneath.
- **The Settings and Calculated tables are framed cards** with a clear header row, and each group of settings gets its own labelled band with a count.
- **Changes since you opened the unit show as coloured pills** (▲ green, ▼ gold) instead of plain text.
