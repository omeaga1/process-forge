# Space grey

- **Dark mode is space grey.** Panels, cards and borders are cool graphite instead of green-black, and the text is brushed silver. Jade stays as the accent, so what's active or passing stands out.
- **The flowsheet canvas sparkles.** A faint starfield and two soft glows sit behind your units, subtle enough not to compete with the drawing.
- **The website matches,** down to the recordings of the studio on the home page.
