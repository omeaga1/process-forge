# A calmer Studio Hub and toolbar

- **The Studio Hub is one column.** Your flowsheets, the templates to start from and your projects now sit in a single readable layout. Templates are tiles with a preview of the line, projects are plain rows with a thin line between them, and "Open a file" is a quiet link.
- **The Account, AI model and Library cards are gone from the Hub.** They said what the header already says. The AI model and Sign in buttons stay in the header, and Library joins them.
- **The canvas toolbar is lighter.** It floats as a soft rounded bar, the running state is shown by its dot instead of a green outline, and only Run is highlighted. Design unit op, Add equipment and Streams are quiet buttons, and the command palette shows a search icon with its shortcut.
- The speed and run-length switches now show the chosen option as a raised chip instead of a green fill.
