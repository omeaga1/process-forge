# The stream table before a run, and what you set vs what was calculated

- **Steady state in the stream table.** Open the stream table before you run, and every stream on a liquid, gas or powder line already has its mass flow, volume flow, temperature and mix, solved from the flowsheet as it is.
- **Your inputs stand out.** Figures you set on a feed (its supply, temperature, mix) are in the accent colour; everything the solver worked out is plain, the way a simulator's stream report shows them.
