# Settings, streams and checks, redone

- **The Settings table is easier to read.** Column titles and group names are plain words instead of small capitals, rows have room to breathe, and the value boxes are lighter. The "Calculated by the engine" table follows the same style.
- **Streams are a simple list.** Each connection shows its name and phase on the left and its design-point figures (temperature, flow, mix, share) on the right, with no coloured edges or boxes. A stream moving in a run adds a "Live in the run" line.
- **Equations show names as text.** Setting names inside an equation are underlined where you can click them, instead of sitting in bordered chips.
- **Checks and Physics read as a list.** Each check is one row: whether it holds, what it guards against, and the settings that move it as plain links. "must hold" and "should hold" replace the bold MUST/SHOULD tags, and Physics lists its governing relations without a box.
